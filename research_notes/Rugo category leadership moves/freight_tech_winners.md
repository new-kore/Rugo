# Freight/Trucking Software Winners 2024–2026: Funding, Growth and Winning Capabilities

Research method note: compiled from web search result summaries plus company and SEC sources (Nov 2026). FreightWaves and valueaddvc.com could not be fetched directly from this session because the network blocked them. Figures attributed to those domains come from search snippets and should be spot-checked. Many metrics are vendor claims, and they are labelled that way.

## Q1. Who raised the most or grew fastest 2024–2026 (TMS, fleet ops, freight AI, freight fintech)?

### Takeaway
The money and growth sit at two extremes. **Fleet-ops platforms** are the scale leaders: Samsara has about $1.9B ARR, and Motive has about $500M+ ARR plus a $1.3B financing in 2026. **AI-agent "digital workforce" startups for brokers and carriers** are the fastest-rising private category: HappyRobot became a unicorn in 2026, Augment raised $85M in its Series A in 2025, and Pallet, Vooma and FleetWorks all raised. Fintech is consolidating into load boards and marketplaces through acquisitions (DAT + Outgo, Truckstop + Denim, Mudflap + Parade) more than through new mega-rounds. Modern TMS players are raising mid-size rounds; the largest is Alvys at $40M.

## Q2. Which capabilities are winning?

### Takeaway
The capabilities attracting the most capital, in order:
1. **AI agents that do phone and email work.** These cover check calls, carrier sales calls and quoting.
2. **AI load building / order entry from emails, tenders and rate confirmations.**
3. **AI safety video plus multi-product fleet platforms.**
4. **Carrier identity and fraud verification.**
5. **Embedded payments, factoring and fuel.** These are increasingly owned by marketplaces.

AI dispatch and load matching for carriers (FleetWorks, Parade) is an emerging fifth area. I found no evidence of large funding tied to emissions/EV reporting in this window.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
**Priorities for a new TMS:**
- **(a) Native or partner AI agents.** Agentic email and voice for intake (load building from tenders and rate confirmations), check calls / tracking, and carrier sales. Incumbents such as McLeod are integrating Augment and Vooma rather than building this, which suggests buyers expect it inside the TMS.
- **(b) Built-in carrier identity verification** through integrations such as Highway and Carrier Assure.
- **(c) Embedded factoring and payments**, likely partnered.
- **(d) An open ELD/telematics integration layer** (Samsara, Motive, Platform Science).

**Why multi-product breadth matters:** Samsara's data (96% of $100K+ customers use 2+ products) shows that platform breadth drives expansion revenue.

### Gaps
- I found no funding or traction data tied specifically to emissions/EV reporting, or to driver retention apps (other than AtoB Driver Pay).
- Document OCR (BOL/POD) is bundled into the agent platforms; I found no standalone funded leaders.

## Q4. How does freight fintech monetize?

### Takeaway
There are three stacked revenue pools:
- **Interchange on fuel and spend cards**, often paired with per-card SaaS.
- **Factoring fees and spread on receivables**, funded by credit facilities.
- **Fuel discount margin.**

Payments and factoring are being bundled into load boards (DAT/Outgo, Truckstop/Denim) and capacity networks (Mudflap/Parade). There they also serve as trust and fraud signals.

### Cited Findings
- AtoB's model is estimated as a "$3–6/card SaaS-plus-interchange stack". This is a third-party estimate; no disclosed interchange figures were found. — [Teardown AtoB](https://www.teardown.ai/companies/atob)
- AtoB's financing mixes equity with large debt components ($80M of debt in 2022; equity plus debt in 2024). This is consistent with funding receivables and credit. — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucking-fintech-startup-atob-raises-155-million-in-series-b-round); [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/paytech/us-transportation-paytech-atob-raises-130m-in-equity-and-debt-funding)
- Outgo's $50M credit facility is used to purchase carrier receivables (factoring). Its "fractional factoring" lets carriers factor individual invoices without long contracts. — [Fintech Global](https://fintech.global/2024/09/25/outgo-accelerates-freight-payment-innovation-with-15m-funding-boost/); [Truck News](https://www.trucknews.com/technology/dat-expands-reach-into-factoring-with-outgo-acquisition/1003197247/)
- Denim's 2022 round was $100M debt and $26M equity, to fund broker payments and working capital. — [Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/freight-fintech-denim-raises-126-million-payments-funding-venture-capital/631994/)
- Mudflap monetizes fuel discounts through its app and fuel card, and bought Parade to connect drivers with broker freight. — [Yahoo/FreightWaves](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/mudflap-acquires-ai-capacity-platform-140000318.html)
- Relay monetizes digital payments at truck stops and lumper payments (Circle K, 500+ locations). — [FleetOwner](https://www.fleetowner.com/news/mergers-acquistions/news/55315607/acquisitions-partnerships-truckstop-relay-payments-and-dhl-strengthen-freight-and-healthcare-solutions)

### Inferences
- For a new TMS, embedded factoring and fuel cards are best done through revenue-share partnerships. The balance-sheet requirement (credit facilities) and the consolidation trend favour partnering over building.
- The TMS can capture take-rate on payments, factoring referrals and card interchange via a partner program issuer.
- Faster carrier pay is also a carrier-acquisition and anti-fraud tool, as seen in the DAT and Highway/Triumph moves.

### Gaps
- No disclosed take-rates were found: factoring fee %, interchange bps or fuel-discount margins.
- No disclosed revenue figures for AtoB, Relay, Mudflap or Denim. AtoB valuation figures conflict across aggregators: $600M, $626M, $700M, and $800M in 2022.
