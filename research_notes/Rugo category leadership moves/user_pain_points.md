# User Pain Points in Trucking TMS and Fleet Software (2024–2026)

Method note: built from web search result summaries (G2/Capterra/TrustRadius/Software Advice listings, BBB, TruckersReport, FreightWaves, ATRI, Trucking HR Canada). Direct page fetches were blocked in this session for ttnews.com, thetruckersreport.com and carrieratlas.com, so several items rely on search-snippet text rather than a full read. Quotes below are short excerpts as surfaced in search snippets. Review-site ratings move over time, so treat numbers as snapshots (late 2026).

## Q1: What users dislike or find missing, by product

### Takeaway
The loudest, most consistent complaints about the two telematics giants (Samsara, Motive) are commercial, not technical: long auto-renewing contracts, price increases mid-term, and hard cancellation. The legacy TMS group (McLeod, Trimble TMW/TruckMate) draws complaints about dated, click-heavy UI, a steep learning curve that needs an in-house "key user", slowness, and paid professional services. Newer cloud TMS (Alvys, AscendTMS, Tai) score well, with thin negatives; no usable evidence was found for Axon, Revenova, Descartes Aljex, Isaac or Geotab.

### Cited Findings
**Samsara**
- BBB lists 84 complaints in the last 3 years. Complaints describe auto-renewal after a 30-day cancellation window, a customer being told Samsara "cannot help unless you buy out your contract", and an "annual quote" that turned out to be a 3-year contract. Samsara's reply in one case cited the expired 30-day cancellation window. — [BBB complaints p1](https://prod.bbb.org/us/ca/san-francisco/profile/electronics-and-technology/samsara-1116-881058/complaints?page=1); [BBB complaints p2](https://prod.bbb.org/us/ca/san-francisco/profile/electronics-and-technology/samsara-1116-881058/complaints?page=2)
- Capterra's AI summary says most reviewers find contract terms "lengthy, inflexible, and contain hidden clauses that complicate cancellation or changes". — [Capterra Samsara reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/167543/Samsara-for-Fleets-0-00-6-23/reviews/)
- Product ratings stay high (about 4.5/5 on G2 from 4,000+ reviews), but Trustpilot is about 3.2/5 from 517 reviews. An aggregator says 2025–26 reviews cluster on billing, auto-renewal, cancellation and removed features. — [checkthat.ai Samsara](https://checkthat.ai/brands/samsara/reviews)
- A competitor blog says the standard contract is 36 months. This comes from a competitor (AirPinpoint) and its exit-fee figures are not corroborated. — [AirPinpoint](https://airpinpoint.com/blog/samsara-lawsuit-contract-trap)
- UK reviews cite cancellation difficulty, auto-renewal surprises, aggressive sales and slow support. — [ExpertSure](https://www.expertsure.com/uk/vehicle-tracking/samsara-review/)
- Drivers on TruckersReport report Samsara "showing wrong hours or just not switching to drive status" and duty-status change problems. These are anecdotal threads. — [TruckersReport thread](https://www.thetruckersreport.com/truckingindustryforum/posts/11699914/); [TruckersReport "Problems with Samsara changing duty status"](https://www.thetruckersreport.com/truckingindustryforum/posts/11883944)
- Privacy: a March 2025 report covers a BIPA lawsuit alleging Samsara captured drivers' facial scans without proper consent. — [Fox News/CyberGuy](https://www.foxnews.com/tech/ai-dashcams-enhance-trucker-safety-while-raising-privacy-concerns)
- A fuel-truck driver fired in August 2025, three days after a warning for cellphone use and "covering the in-cab camera", is suing over in-cab Samsara monitoring under California law. — [CDLLife 2026](https://cdllife.com/2026/fired-fuel-truck-driver-sues-over-whistleblower-retaliation-in-cab-monitoring-and-racy-calendars-in-company-trucks/)

**Motive (KeepTruckin)**
- A Capterra reviewer said a 2025 price-increase email led them to cancel, and they were refused a refund for unused months. Another said they were charged after the increase. — [Capterra Motive reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/138669/KeepTruckin/reviews/)
- A Trustpilot reviewer said they signed in 2022 believing it was a 3-year deal, but the paperwork said 4 years. Another reported that in November 2025 Motive suspended a driver app over alleged non-payment while the dashboard stayed active. — [Trustpilot Motive](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/gomotive.com?page=3)
- A May 2025 TruckersReport thread is titled "Can't cancel my motive account". An aggregator cites 215 BBB complaints in 3 years, 64 unanswered. This BBB figure is unverified, so check it on bbb.org. — [checkthat.ai Motive](https://checkthat.ai/brands/motive/reviews)
- Motive is rated about 89% positive on Capterra (1,687 reviews), with praise for ELD compliance and the UI. Complaints mention bugs and connection issues, a 12-month minimum contract and being "expensive compared to competitors". — [Capterra Motive](https://capterra.com/p/138669/Motive/); [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/motive/reviews)
- CarrierAtlas ran a story headlined "Motive throttled Highway ELD data after payment demand". This is a dispute over data access for a third-party carrier-vetting partner. Details were not verified because the page could not be fetched. — [CarrierAtlas](https://carrieratlas.com/news/motive-throttled-highway-eld-data-after-payment-demand)
- Motive's own help center documents unidentified trips that don't appear in the driver app because of "a temporary sync issue or connection glitch", and conflicts between manual and ELD drive time that block claiming trips. — [Motive help: troubleshooting](https://helpcenter.gomotive.com/hc/en-us/articles/17854077026589); [Motive help: conflicting trips](https://helpcenter.gomotive.com/hc/en-us/articles/6162557552285)
- A TruckersReport thread is titled "Motive Driver e-logs. The new wild west?" (anecdotal). — [TruckersReport](https://www.thetruckersreport.com/truckingindustryforum/posts/12006305)

**McLeod LoadMaster / PowerBroker**
- Users describe it as "outdated and not very user friendly". — [SoftwareConnect](https://softwareconnect.com/reviews/mcleod-loadmaster/)
- A G2 review is titled "Outdated Interface and Glitches Undermine Useful Load Management". — [G2 LoadMaster](https://www.g2.com/products/loadmaster/reviews)
- A reviewer said list search results "can't be exported to an Excel spreadsheet", and another said running large reports "can make the program crash or lag". — [G2 LoadMaster](https://www.g2.com/products/loadmaster/reviews)
- Capterra: ease of use 3.1/5, overall 3.7/5 (16 reviews). Reviewers cite the "high level of expertise needed as its not that friendly user interface" and the need for a trained internal key user. — [Capterra LoadMaster](https://www.capterra.com/p/16847/LoadMaster/reviews/)
- TrustRadius gives 7.5/10 (4 reviews). One reviewer wanted a fresher look: "it is not ugly but McLeod is strong [and] powerful". — [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/mcleod-loadmaster/reviews)
- Positives include data depth and integration with McLeod Driver Sidekick. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/loadmaster/reviews)

**Trimble TMS (TMW.Suite / TruckMate)**
- Capterra: 3.7/5 overall (38 reviews), ease of use 3.4, support 3.8. One reviewer said "biggest con … is its speed and click-intensiveness". Others cite "way too many pop ups", "slow and requires manual refresh to prevent crashes", and "files locking up orders and equipment". — [Capterra Trimble TMS](https://www.capterra.com/p/175186/Transportation-Management-and-Trucking-Software/reviews/)
- On support, one reviewer gave tech support "'A' for effort but 'F' for functionality and follow through". Another said "sometimes support requires professional service which cost money", and documentation is lacking or outdated. — [Software Advice Trimble TMS](https://www.softwareadvice.com/cmms/trimble-tms-profile/reviews/); [SoftwareConnect TMWSuite](https://softwareconnect.com/reviews/trimble-tmwsuite/)
- Cost: a starting price of about $20,000 for a perpetual license is cited, the CRM add-on is called "expensive", and value for money is 3.3/5. Caveat: several negative reviews date from 2017–2020. — [SoftwareConnect](https://softwareconnect.com/reviews/trimble-tmwsuite/); [Capterra TMWSuite](https://www.capterra.ca/software/1035038/tmwsuite)

**Cloud-native and SMB TMS**
- Alvys: 4.4/5 (51 reviews), ease of use 4.6, support 4.5. One accountant noted that the dashboard and customer revenue figures don't match. — [Capterra Alvys](https://capterra.com/p/249961/Alvys-TMS/reviews/)
- Rose Rocket is now listed on Capterra as "TMS.AI" (updated March 2026). No review-level cons were retrieved. — [Capterra TMS.AI](https://capterra.com/p/153047/Rose-Rocket/)
- AscendTMS: 4.9/5 (366 reviews), with 1 negative. Missing features include posting office expenses. Note that it is broker-oriented. — [Capterra AscendTMS](https://www.capterra.com/p/122488/AscendTMS-Logistics-Software/reviews/); [SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/supply-chain-management/ascendtms/reviews)
- TruckingOffice: about 4.5 (30 reviews). No written cons found. — [SoftwareReview](https://softwarereview.com/s/ascendtms-logistics-software); [GetApp compare](https://www.getapp.com/transportation-logistics-software/a/truckingoffice/compare/ascendtms/)
- Tai TMS: cons are that referring partners onto Tai "creates extra barriers and less support for them", and onboarding was difficult. — [Software Advice Tai](https://www.softwareadvice.com/scm/teknowlogi-tms-profile/reviews/)
- ITS Dispatch (SMB TMS): "Lots of double entry, did not integrate with Quickbooks Online or Geotab." — [Capterra ITS Dispatch](https://www.capterra.com/p/106760/ITS-Dispatch/reviews/)

**Isaac Instruments (Canada-heavy) / Geotab**
- At Isaac's user conference, drivers were reported to "initially don't like it", with some covering tablets with Scotch tape or putting tissues over road-facing cameras. — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/the-quest-to-get-truck-drivers-to-stop-worrying-and-love-the-eld)
- A forum user said Isaac "does have some glitches but we have a customized install … dispatch is implemented into it". This is anecdotal and of unclear date. — [TruckersReport ELD favorites](https://www.thetruckersreport.com/truckingindustryforum/posts/12439522)

### Inferences
- The clearest opening against Samsara/Motive is commercial trust: month-to-month or annual terms, no auto-renew traps, self-serve cancellation, and price locks. Their product quality is not where they are weak.
- Legacy TMS (McLeod, Trimble) is defended by depth and switching cost. Its weak points are UX speed (fewer clicks), self-serve reporting and export, onboarding without a dedicated in-house expert, and support that isn't billed as professional services.
- Review counts for mid-market and legacy TMS are tiny (4–40 reviews), so forum and community evidence matters more than star ratings for these products.

### Gaps
- No usable review evidence was found for Axon, Revenova, Descartes (Aljex/MacroPoint), Geotab (driver app) or Isaac beyond the items above. Searches returned unrelated results.
- Reddit threads (r/Truckers, r/FreightBrokers) did not surface in search. Direct fetches of TruckersReport, TT News and CarrierAtlas were blocked by the session network, so full quotes and dates could not be pulled.
- No official Motive statement on 2025 pricing was found.

## Q2: Top workflow pain points regardless of vendor

### Takeaway
Detention is the best-quantified pain: 39% of stops, about $15B a year, and carriers collect on less than half of detention invoices. Double entry, check calls and paperwork-gated invoicing (payment terms of 30–60+ days, with the clock starting at signed POD) are the core daily drag. Small carriers largely still run on spreadsheets, email and paper.

### Cited Findings
- Detention occurred at 39.3% of 2023 stops, and 9.9% lasted more than 2 hours. It cost $3.6B in direct expenses plus $11.5B in lost productivity, and more than 135M hours. — [ATRI Detention report, Sept 2024](https://truckingresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/ATRI-Costs-and-Consequences-of-Truck-Driver-Detention-09-2024.pdf); [ATRI release](https://truckingresearch.org/2024/09/new-research-documents-substantial-financial-and-safety-impacts-from-truck-driver-detention/)
- 52% of truckload drivers ran out of HOS because of detention, and detained trucks ran 14.6% faster afterward. 94.5% of carriers charge detention at least sometimes, but they are paid on less than half of those invoices. — [ATRI Detention report](https://truckingresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/ATRI-Costs-and-Consequences-of-Truck-Driver-Detention-09-2024.pdf); [MHL News](https://www.mhlnews.com/transportation-distribution/article/55140053/truck-driver-detention-a-big-problem)
- Double entry and check calls are named as the workflow problem: "any workflow where a human has to manually move information between systems", along with manual check calls and retyped status updates. This is vendor commentary. — [Loadstop](https://loadstop.com/blog/category/ntegrated-logistics)
- Board searching, broker calls, check calls and data entry limit how many trucks one dispatcher can run. This is vendor commentary. — [Bubba.ai](https://bubba.ai/resources/trucks-per-dispatcher)
- An ITS Dispatch user wanted carrier contract signing built in "to avoid double entry data". — [Capterra ITS Dispatch p2](https://www.capterra.com/p/106760/ITS-Dispatch/reviews/?page=2)
- Payment terms run 30–60 days, and 30–90 for owner-operators. One guide cites an average of about 33 days on Net 30, and one broker averaging 42 days. These are blog/vendor figures. — [OTR Solutions](https://otrsolutions.com/blog/delayed-payments-in-trucking); [otrucking guide](https://otrucking.com/resources/guides/broker-payment-terms/)
- The payment clock typically starts on a complete invoice plus signed POD, and missing documents "freeze" payment. Quick pay costs about 1.5–5% for payment in 1–7 days, and factoring about 1–5% (2–3% typical). — [TrueNorth](https://www.truenorth.com/articles/business/get-paid-faster-by-freight-brokers); [otrucking](https://otrucking.com/resources/guides/broker-payment-terms/)
- A TruckersReport thread asks "What's the most frustrating part of getting paid after a load?" — [TruckersReport](https://www.thetruckersreport.com/truckingindustryforum/posts/13223672)
- Unassigned drive time is a carrier compliance burden, with some fleets generating thousands of unassigned events per day. — [Fleet Equipment (NFI)](https://www.fleetequipmentmag.com/truck-unassigned-drive-time/)

### Inferences
- Automating detention capture, from geofence timestamps through to a billable accessorial with proof attached to the invoice, addresses a quantified multi-billion-dollar leak.
- POD-to-invoice same day, with document capture in the driver app feeding invoice and factoring automatically, directly attacks cash-flow delay.
- Native ELD, TMS and accounting sync (QuickBooks, Geotab/Samsara/Motive) without re-keying is table stakes that SMB tools still miss.

### Gaps
- No 2024–26 survey quantifying POD/paperwork-driven payment delay was found. IFTA/fuel tax pain specifically was not evidenced.

## Q3: What drivers complain about in fleet and ELD apps

### Takeaway
Drivers' strongest objection is surveillance (AI driver-facing cameras, biometric capture), followed by log accuracy glitches (wrong duty status, unassigned drive time). Their top industry issues are pay, parking and English proficiency, not software.

### Cited Findings
- An ATRI study found drivers rate driver-facing cameras 2.24 on a 0–10 approval scale. Women raised privacy concerns 34% more than men. — [TT News (cameras/driver acceptance)](https://ttnews.com/articles/cameras-driver-acceptance); [Fox News summary](https://www.foxnews.com/tech/ai-dashcams-enhance-trucker-safety-while-raising-privacy-concerns)
- Lytx settled a BIPA suit for more than $4M, brought by driver Joshua Lewis against Lytx and Maverick Transportation. — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/?p=524007)
- On a TruckersReport "AI Driver Facing Cameras, how's it going?" thread, one driver said they couldn't find a trucking job without cameras and "finally decided to quit trucking" (a single anecdote). — [TruckersReport](https://www.thetruckersreport.com/truckingindustryforum/threads/ai-driver-facing-cameras-how%E2%80%99s-it-going.2502636/page-3)
- At Ryder, drivers initially threatened to quit over cameras, but a "negligible" number did. — [Fleet Equipment](https://www.fleetequipmentmag.com/fleet-experience-driver-facing-cameras/)
- Canadian coverage: "Truckers not fans of driver-facing cameras due to privacy concerns". — [Trucknews.com](https://www.trucknews.com/technology/truckers-not-fans-of-driver-facing-cameras-due-to-privacy-concerns/1003174233)
- Samsara drivers report wrong hours and failures to switch to drive status, and Motive drivers report e-log disputes. — [TruckersReport Samsara](https://www.thetruckersreport.com/truckingindustryforum/posts/11699914/); [TruckersReport Motive](https://www.thetruckersreport.com/truckingindustryforum/posts/12006305)
- In ATRI 2025, drivers were 30% of about 4,600 respondents. Their top three issues were driver compensation, truck parking and English language proficiency. — [Platform Science summary](https://www.platformscience.com/blog/exploring-atris-top-10-issues-in-trucking-for-2025); [TruckersNews](https://truckersnews.com/home/article/15770250/top-issues-survey-economy-is-top-concern-overall-drivers-say-its-pay)
- Some drivers cover tablets and cameras with tape or tissues. — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/the-quest-to-get-truck-drivers-to-stop-worrying-and-love-the-eld)

### Inferences
- A driver-first design (exoneration-first video, driver-controlled privacy modes when off-duty, transparent event review) plus pay transparency (settlement visibility, detention pay tracking) would answer what drivers actually care about.

### Gaps
- No systematic app-store rating analysis of the Motive, Samsara or Geotab Drive apps was done. Driver quotes could not be fully extracted from blocked forum pages.

## Q4: What shippers and brokers complain about

### Takeaway
Shippers say visibility from providers is insufficient: only about 1 in 5 think it is enough, and confidence in data and ETA accuracy is low. Evidence specific to 2025 on appointment scheduling and tracking-link complaints is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Only 1 in 5 shippers agree their logistics providers offer sufficient visibility (FreightWaves/Tive survey; date not confirmed in snippet). — [Tive survey report](https://www.tive.com/content/survey-report-shippers-dissatisfied-by-lack-of-shipment-visibility); [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/white-paper-logistics-service-providers-have-some-work-to-do-shippers-dissatisfied-by-lack-of-shipment-visibility)
- Only 27% of shippers are confident in the accuracy of their data, and fewer than a third believe they have end-to-end visibility. — [The Loadstar](https://theloadstar.com/supply-chain-visibility-roi-in-2025-what-buyers-should-know/)
- ETA accuracy is "lagging" per survey respondents (older, 2022). — [Logistics Viewpoints](https://logisticsviewpoints.com/2022/03/23/real-time-visibilit/)
- Visibility is "an expectation that most brokers struggle to meet". — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/many-shippers-underestimate-visibility-expectations)
- 37% of companies can't track in-transit cargo. — [MHL News](https://www.mhlnews.com/transportation-distribution/news/55272257/37-of-companies-cant-track-in-transit-cargo)
- A June 2025 Denim survey of about 100 shippers found brokers and carriers are evaluated on more than cost. — [Denim](https://www.denim.com/news/freight-survey-reveals-what-shippers-really-want-from-brokers-and-carriers)
- A Werner 2026 white paper ties visibility gaps to service failures and cargo theft concerns. — [Werner 2026 white paper](https://www.werner.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/26-105-Werner_2026_white_paper_V3.pdf)
- Unverified: a claim that "73% of shippers would switch over poor visibility" (attributed to Descartes) appears only on an unreliable blog. Do not use it. — [freightpulse blog](https://freightpulse.pages.dev/blog/real-time-freight-visibility-tracking-2026)

### Inferences
- A carrier platform that auto-shares live ETA and tracking links (with no driver app install burden) and pushes status into broker and shipper TMS via API/EDI removes check calls on both sides.

### Gaps
- No 2024–26 data was found on appointment scheduling complaints, tracking link fatigue (MacroPoint/Trucker Tools) or broker-side TMS complaints (Revenova, Aljex).

## Q5: Surveys and stats

### Takeaway
ATRI 2025 ranks the economy first, then lawsuit abuse, insurance, truck parking and driver compensation. US for-hire turnover fell to about 44% in 2025 but stays 70%+ at large truckload carriers. Small-carrier TMS adoption is low (roughly 15–33% by older and vendor estimates). Canadian driver vacancies eased to a 3.5% rate in Q1 2025.

### Cited Findings
- ATRI 2025 Top Industry Issues (about 4,600 respondents): 1. economy, 2. lawsuit abuse reform, 3. insurance cost, 4. truck parking, 5. driver compensation. New to the list are diesel emissions rules, English proficiency, driver training standards and AI. — [TT News](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/atri-trucking-issues-2025); [SupplyChain247](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/top-trucking-issues-2025-atri-report); [CCJ](https://ccjdigital.com/business/article/15769794/economy-regulations-boot-driver-shortage-off-list-of-industry-problems)
- US for-hire turnover was about 44.2% in 2025, down from 48% in 2024 (ATRI). Large truckload was 70.7% and small specialized 13.9%. Private fleets run about 17.1% (NPTC). — [FleetOwner](https://www.fleetowner.com/operations/article/55417849/trucking-by-the-numbers-2026-for-hire-private-and-driver-stats)
- The long-run large truckload rate is about 92.7% annualized (1996–2023, ATA via National Academies). OOIDA's 2025 paper calls this "churn", not a shortage. — [OOIDA whitepaper](https://www.ooida.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/The-Churn-A-Brief-Look-at-the-Roots-of-High-Driver-Turnover-in-U.S.-Trucking.pdf); [Overdrive](https://www.overdriveonline.com/business/article/15744481/new-research-takes-aim-at-driver-shortage-narrative-tracing-roots-to-deregulation)
- TMS adoption (older): 91% of carriers with 20+ trucks use a TMS, versus 33% with 10 or fewer and 17% with fewer than 5 (2018 InMotion survey). A 2018 CarrierLists survey of 5–50 truck fleets found about 30%. — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freight-industry-still-lags-in-technology-adoption)
- Newer vendor estimate: fewer than 15% of small carriers use a dedicated TMS and they run on spreadsheets, email and paper (Numeo 2026, vendor). AlphaLoops reports TMS at 0.1% for 1–35 truck carriers versus 67% for 250+. The methods conflict, so treat these as directional. — [Numeo](https://numeo.ai/blog/state-of-carrier-technology-2026); [AlphaLoops](https://runalphaloops.com/research/tms-landscape)
- Small carriers moving from spreadsheets to TMS are covered by TT News. — [TT News](https://ttnews.com/articles/small-growing-carriers-make-leap-spreadsheets-tms-software)
- Canada: the Q1 2025 driver vacancy rate was 3.5% (from 4.6%), about 13,900 vacancies, down 24% year over year (peak above 28,000 in 2021–22). Rising costs have overtaken the driver shortage as employers' top concern. Dispatchers and IT specialists are in demand. — [Trucking HR Canada Q1 2025](https://truckinghr.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/National-Job-Vacancy-update-Q1-2025.pdf); [Trucknews](https://www.trucknews.com/transportation/hiring-pressures-continue-to-ease-in-trucking-and-logistics-thrc-says/1003200504); [Trucking HR Canada](https://truckinghr.com/2025/09/02/labour-challenges-are-now-opportunities/)

### Inferences
- The SMB carrier segment (under 10–35 trucks) is largely un-penetrated by TMS. The competitor there is spreadsheets, QuickBooks and the ELD vendor's add-ons, so ease of onboarding and price matter more than feature depth.
- With cost pressure as the top concern (ATRI economy and insurance; THRC costs), ROI framing (detention recovered, days-sales-outstanding cut, dispatcher capacity) will resonate more than features.

### Gaps
- No ATRI 2026 top-issues data was retrieved. No CCJ or FreightWaves carrier software satisfaction survey from 2024–26 was found.
- Canadian voices on specific software (Isaac, TruckMate, Geotab) are thin. Trucking News and Today's Trucking pages on software complaints were not surfaced.
